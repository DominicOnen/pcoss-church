Presbyterian Church of South Sudan (PCOSS)

Equatoria Presbytery — Juba Parish

Serving the Lord at Hai Matar through worship in English and Arabic, discipleship, and online Bible study.

Our Location & Services

Worship at Hai Matar

Juba Parish holds two main worship services every Sunday at our Hai Matar sanctuary:

  • English Service: 7:45 AM – 9:45 AM
  • Arabic Service: 10:00 AM – 12:30 PM

We also host interactive weekly Bible studies online via Google Meet every Wednesday evening from 8:00 PM to 9:00 PM.

Worship & Music Ministry

Choir Rehearsal Schedule

Our choir and music teams prepare throughout the week to lead our congregation in praising God:

  • Mondays: Music Training
  • Tuesdays: Arabic Choir Rehearsal
  • Wednesdays: Band Practice
  • Fridays: Arabic Final Sound Check
  • Saturdays: English Final Sound Check
